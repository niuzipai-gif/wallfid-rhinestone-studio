MAKING GUIDE

From picture to piece.

  1. Choose the board from your kit. The 50 mm board suits simple motifs; 100 mm offers more detail.
  2. Add a clear image and frame your subject. Small patterns simplify photographs.
  3. Create the pattern. Choose Numbers to see the placement guide.
  4. Select a colour number to highlight it in the pattern and find its compartment. Number 1 starts at the top left, with 8 compartments in each row.
  5. Print at actual size / 100%, with headers and footers off. Measure the 50 mm scale line.
  6. Remove the protective film and secure the checked template beneath the board. Follow the kit’s adhesive and curing instructions.

This is a design preview. Photo-derived colours, spacing, corner shape and kit quantities still need to be checked with physical samples.
