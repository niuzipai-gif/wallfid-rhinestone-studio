MADE BY HAND. SHOWN STEP BY STEP.

Your first stone starts here.

Prepare a clean, dry surface. For acrylic, remove the protective film and secure your checked, actual-size pattern underneath.

Tap a photo to see the detail

  1. 01Apply a little glue

    Apply a small amount to the area you want to decorate. Work on a small section at a time.

  2. 02Find the fine round tip

    Use the narrow metal end with the small rounded tip shown here.

  3. 03Load the silicone putty

    Press the fine tip into the silicone putty and turn it once to pick up a little putty.

  4. 04Check the prepared tip

    A small amount of putty should cover the rounded end, as shown in the close-up.

  5. 05Pick up one rhinestone

    Touch the putty to the faceted top of one stone. Keep its flat back facing the glued surface.

  6. 06Place, press and lift

    Match your pattern and place the whole flat back on the glued board, clear of its edges. Press gently, lift the pen, then let the glue cure.

Silicone putty helps the pen pick up stones. Glue fixes the stones to your surface. Follow the adhesive label for use and curing time.

MAKING GUIDE

From picture to piece.

For your own cup, notebook or other surface. Measure the area first. Sizes from 12 to 600 mm.

  1. Choose the board from your kit. The 50 mm board suits simple motifs; 100 mm offers more detail.
  2. Add a clear image and frame your subject. Small patterns simplify photographs.
  3. Create the pattern. Choose Numbers to see the placement guide.
  4. Select a colour number to highlight it in the pattern and find its compartment. Number 1 starts at the top left, with 8 compartments in each row.
  5. Print at actual size / 100%, with headers and footers off. Measure the 50 mm scale line.
  6. Remove the protective film and secure the checked template beneath the board. Follow the kit’s adhesive and curing instructions.

3 mm round stones. Supplied acrylic boards have square corners. Colours are photo estimates; check your kit.
